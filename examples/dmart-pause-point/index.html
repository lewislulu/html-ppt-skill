03 — THE INSIGHT
DMART × PAUSE POINT
HCD MODULE, SEM 3 · GROUP 7
03 / 10 · INSIGHT
They already lean on the trolley.
So we made the trolley the seat.
01 A SEAT AREA LOSES THE ARGUMENT
Fixed seating eats the aisle space the store does not have, and crowds a billing zone that is already tight.
02 THE TROLLEY IS ALREADY THE REST POINT
Shoppers rest on trolleys, shelves, the lift and the stairs. The trolley is the only one that travels the whole trip with them.
03 SO THE FIX COSTS NO FLOOR SPACE
No new aisle, no new staff, no new habit — a seat on the cart she is already holding.
THE REFRAME
Add a seating area
Costs aisle space in the busiest zone of the store.
Put the seat on the trolley
Costs nothing in floor space, and it moves with the shopper.
From our in-store observation and empathy map: anything that takes their weight for a minute becomes a seat.

Pushing back on the seat pushes the cart forward. The answer is now a wheel brake: it locks the cart and carries the user’s weight.

06 / 10 · PRODUCT
Pause Point: a fold-out seat on the trolley, so a tired shopper can finish the trip.
A low, firm seat that folds out of the trolley’s lower bar and locks the wheels — a short rest in the aisle or the queue, without letting go of the basket.
01 FOLD
One hand, no attendant. The paddle drops from the lower bar to a 1.5–2 in seat.
02 LOCK AND SIT
The brake holds the wheels, so her weight stays on the seat and the cart stays put.
03 STOW
Sit out the queue, fold it flat, pay, walk out. Nothing to hand back.
